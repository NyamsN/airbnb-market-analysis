Week 1 at AnalystLab Africa done! 🏠📊

As a Data Science intern, I investigated what drives advertised Airbnb prices across 10 cities using 279,712 listings and 5.37 million review records.

What I found:
• Room type separates prices in every city: an entire place advertises 1.5–2.6× a private room
• Each extra guest of capacity goes with roughly 7–23% higher advertised price, even after controlling for room type and bedrooms
• Review volume is not a quality signal: listings with few reviews have the most extreme ratings

The biggest lesson: prices were in 8 currencies with no currency field, so every test had to run within each city. Clean data decisions matter as much as the statistics.

Tools: Python, pandas, SciPy, statsmodels, Seaborn.
